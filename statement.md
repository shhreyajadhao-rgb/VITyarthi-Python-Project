# Project Statement

## Project Title

Online Mall Management System

## Problem Statement

Managing products, inventory, shopping carts, and purchases manually can be
time-consuming and difficult to manage.

The Online Mall Management System provides a simple console-based solution
for performing basic online shopping and inventory management operations.

The system allows customers to browse products, manage their shopping cart,
and complete purchases. It also allows the owner to manage product inventory.

## Scope of the Project

The project focuses on the basic operations of an online shopping mall.

The system includes:

- Product categorization
- Inventory management
- Customer shopping
- Shopping cart management
- Product quantity validation
- Checkout
- Inventory updates
- JSON-based data storage

The current project is a console-based application.

## Target Users

### Customers

Customers can:

- Browse products
- View prices and available quantities
- Add products to their cart
- Remove products from their cart
- View their cart
- Complete purchases

### Owner

The owner can:

- View inventory
- Refill products
- Add new products
- Add new categories
- Maintain product quantities

## High-Level Features

1. Customer shopping
2. Product category browsing
3. Shopping cart management
4. Checkout
5. Inventory management
6. Product management
7. Category management
8. JSON-based inventory storage

## Technical Approach

The project is implemented using Python.

Dictionaries are used to represent product categories and product information,
while lists are used to manage the shopping cart.

Functions are used to organize the different operations of the application.

JSON file handling is used to store inventory information persistently.

## Expected Outcome

The project provides a functional console-based simulation of an online mall
where customers can purchase products and owners can manage inventory.
